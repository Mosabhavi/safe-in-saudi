# Safe in Saudi

Wartime safety instructions for people living in Saudi Arabia who don't read Arabic, in English, Tagalog, Urdu, Hindi and Bengali.

Every instruction comes from official Saudi statements (Ministry of Interior, Ministry of Defense, Civil Defense), linked at the bottom of the page. This is a volunteer project and is not affiliated with any government agency.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the whole site in one file. Open `index.html#tl`, `#ur`, `#hi` or `#bn` to start in that language.
- `posters/`: one shareable poster per language, with a QR code that opens the site in that language.

## Covered

- The three siren tones and phone alerts
- What to do inside a building, outside, or while driving
- Never filming or sharing interceptions or debris (legal warning)
- Reporting suspicious aerial sightings in Tawakkalna
- Debris: stay away, call Civil Defense on 998
- Official news sources, embassy links, official ways to help
- A three-question check

Last checked against official sources: 12 October 2026. If official guidance changes, the official guidance wins.
